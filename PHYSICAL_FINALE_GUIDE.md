# Physical Finale Continuity Notes

This creator-only document is not displayed by the website.

The digital finale should end quietly at “Lift the tray.” Beneath the removable six-compartment wooden tray, arrange pressed flowers and an ivory envelope with a deep burgundy wax seal. A restrained envelope line such as “Some things are worth saying.” connects naturally to the promise correspondence without exposing the letter online.

The handwritten letter can move through:

1. The escape-room first date and why that beginning still makes you smile.
2. What the last two years have meant, using only true shared details.
3. Why a handmade collection felt like the right way to do this properly.
4. The time and experiences you would like to share without presuming her choices.
5. The consistency, effort, respect, and dependability you intend to bring.
6. Mutual consideration expressed as a hope, not a list of conditions.

A restrained version of the mutual-standard thought is: “I know what I want to bring into this—consistency, effort, respect, and someone you can count on. And I want us to give each other that same consideration.”

The intended final handwritten instruction is “Look up at me.” It must remain on paper, never in recipient-facing website copy. Once she reaches the letter, the website has no further action, question, response form, sound, or celebration. The real conversation takes over.
